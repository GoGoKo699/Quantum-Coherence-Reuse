# Model and uniform resource law

The task is to supply independent coherent qubits while returning the phase reference that makes their preparation possible. Accuracy is imposed on the **whole output**, including the reference and every produced qubit. The resource optimized here is the reference's initial mean energy above its spectral lower bound.

## Registers, input and target

Set the qubit energy gap to one and write

$$
Q_N=\sum_{j=1}^{N}|1\rangle\langle1|_j,
\qquad
|+\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle+|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Throughout, $N\ge2$ is even. The supplied qubits start in the known state

$$
\eta_N=(|01\rangle\langle01|)^{\otimes N/2}.
$$

This state has exactly $N/2$ excitations and is invariant under the time translations generated by $Q_N$. The desired output is

$$
\psi_N=(|+\rangle\langle+|)^{\otimes N}.
$$

The reference $R$ has a self-adjoint Hamiltonian $H_R\ge0$, with energy origin at its spectral infimum, and an initial density operator $\sigma_R$ satisfying

$$
E=\mathrm{Tr}(H_R\sigma_R)<\infty.
$$

The reference state is supplied and known, and is initially independent of the qubits. The converse permits finite or infinite-dimensional references, arbitrary degeneracies, sparse or continuous spectra, mixed states, and energy distributions with infinite second moment. There is no requirement that zero be an eigenvalue. Ordinary trace-class quantum states and their spectral energy distributions are meant; no assertion about general operator-algebraic states is needed.

## Allowed operations

An allowed global protocol is a completely positive trace-preserving map $\Lambda$ on $RS$ satisfying time-translation covariance:

$$
\Lambda\left(U_t\rho U_t^\dagger\right)
=U_t\Lambda(\rho)U_t^\dagger
\quad\text{for every real }t,
\qquad
U_t=e^{-it(H_R+Q_N)}.
$$

This is a symmetry restriction on the complete protocol. It includes operations assembled from exactly conserving interactions, initially invariant ancillary states and invariant discarding or classical records. The entire auxiliary input must be jointly invariant and independent of $RS$; invariant individual marginals alone do not establish that condition. An additional phase reference in the apparatus or controls is not free. Any initially asymmetric system that is used must be included in the counted reference. Classical randomness without an asymmetric physical carrier does not evade this condition.

For a general reference spectrum the group is the real time-translation group. The finite integer-ladder construction also has the usual $U(1)$ description. Covariance by itself does not require conservation of mean energy: an invariant environment can supply or absorb energy. The converse therefore covers a larger operational class than closed conserving unitaries.

## Joint error and return

Let

$$
D(\rho,\tau)=\frac12\Vert \rho-\tau\Vert _1
$$

be half trace distance. The actual error is

$$
\epsilon(\sigma_R,\Lambda;N)
=D\left(
\Lambda(\sigma_R\otimes\eta_N),
\sigma_R\otimes\psi_N
\right).
$$

Thus the comparison returns the **same initial reference state** and makes it independent of the complete product of outputs. It controls every subsequent joint statistical test on those registers. Good one-qubit marginals, a good final reference marginal, or a bound obtained after discarding old outputs does not imply this contract.

The mixed reference is a known state, rather than unknown borrowed quantum data. The definition does not demand identity action on every possible reference input or restoration of an inaccessible purification. The lower bound already holds for this weaker requirement. The attaining reference is pure, so the upper bound also satisfies the version with an unchanged inaccessible purification.

## Resource optimization

Define

$$
E_{\min}(N,\delta)
=\inf\left\lbrace
\mathrm{Tr}(H_R\sigma_R):
\epsilon(\sigma_R,\Lambda;N)\le\delta
\right\rbrace ,
$$

where the infimum ranges over the allowed reference systems, states and covariant protocols just specified. They may be chosen for the requested $N$ and $\delta$. An infimum need not be attained; the theorem provides finite explicit upper-bound witnesses rather than a claim that a global optimizer exists.

### Uniform theorem

For every even $N\ge2$ and $0<\delta\le1/8$,

$$
\boxed{
\frac{1}{32\sqrt2}\sqrt{N/\delta}
\le E_{\min}(N,\delta)+1
\le3\sqrt{N/\delta}.
}
$$

The additive one can be removed without changing the uniform regime. In particular, the following loose constants also hold:

$$
\boxed{
\frac1{1024}\sqrt{N/\delta}
\le E_{\min}(N,\delta)
\le3\sqrt{N/\delta}.
}
$$

Consequently $E_{\min}(N,\delta)=\Theta(\sqrt{N/\delta})$ with constants independent of both $N$ and $\delta$ in this domain. This is a joint two-parameter statement, not an exchange of a fixed $N$ limit with a growing-system limit. Exact return with exact coherent output is impossible at finite $E$, including for an infinite-dimensional reference of finite mean energy.

The [converse](converse.md) proves the lower bounds for arbitrary mixed finite-mean references and arbitrary covariant channels. The [construction](construction.md) supplies a finite ladder with a smooth pure reference and exactly conserving sector rotations. The [sequential protocol](sequential_reuse.md) attains the same order by repeatedly applying **one fixed reference–pair unitary** to fresh copies of $|01\rangle$; its processor depends on the chosen reference size but not on the eventual number of calls. Every previous output remains in the error comparison.

## Constants from the finite construction

For an integer $L\ge3$, the attaining reference has $L-1$ levels with energies $0,\ldots,L-2$ and mean energy

$$
E_L=\frac L2-1.
$$

The cited construction and sequential hybrid give

$$
\epsilon\le C\frac N{L^2},
\qquad
C=\frac{7\pi^2}{\sqrt6}<29.
$$

Taking $L=\lceil\sqrt{29N/\delta}\rceil$ gives an allowed finite witness. Since $\sqrt{N/\delta}\ge4$ in the theorem's domain,

$$
E_L+1=\frac L2
\le\frac{\sqrt{29}}2\sqrt{N/\delta}+\frac12
<3\sqrt{N/\delta}.
$$

These constants are sufficient bounds, not optimized prefactors.

## What is optimized, and what is not

The converse allows one joint covariant operation, auxiliary invariant resources and arbitrary control complexity. The upper bound uses the more restricted fixed pair interface. Matching their energy orders proves that this repeated-pair interface is sufficient for the global optimum **in reference mean-energy order**. It does not say that the explicit processor is the uniquely optimal unitary or that its exact finite error is globally optimal.

This is known-state preparation with a covariantly carried phase. If the input reference is rotated, the output target is rotated by the same amount. It is not an arbitrary unknown-input gate, an independent phase standard, or a source that starts every qubit in its ground state. The supplied qubit energy $N/2$, reference preparation, number-selective control, timing, locality and other costs are distinguished in [resource accounting](resource_accounting.md).
