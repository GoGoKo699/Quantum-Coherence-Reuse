# Learn Quantum Coherence Reuse

The central question is whether a finite phase reference can supply many coherent qubits while the reference and the entire output batch remain close to an independent ideal product. The resource law is about initial reference mean energy, with the input pairs' excitation supplied separately.

The single educational anchor is **John Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information* (Cambridge University Press, 2018)**, using the [author's available manuscript](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/). Only selected foundations from Chapters 1–3 are needed. The book is not a prerequisite to read in full, and its manuscript is not redistributed here.

The milestone map describes shared understanding targets rather than a reader's assessment or progress. The tutorial connects those targets to a concrete source and to the complete theorem. The bridge translates the book's norms and fidelity convention and supplies the conservation, first-moment, Hellinger and reuse arguments beyond its selected sections. Primary research papers remain necessary for attribution, as explained in [related work](../references/RELATED_WORK.md); they are not additional educational anchors.

Keep four distinctions visible throughout: the complete joint error versus individual marginals, the reference's mean energy versus total supplied energy, a finite construction versus the broad mixed-reference converse, and an explicit matrix description versus efficient hardware control.
